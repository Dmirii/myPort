Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data Controller: Dmitry Antonov (Private Individual).
Contact email: dimaa@dimaa.ru

1. What Data We Collect

This website collects two categories of data:

A) Information you provide voluntarily:

  • Your email address — when submitting the notification form for upcoming book releases or feedback
  • (Optional) Specific book or inquiry details

B) Automatically collected technical data (web analytics):

  • Anonymized IP address
  • Device type, browser version, and operating system
  • Referrer URL (source of arrival)
  • Viewed pages, clicks, and session duration

This technical data is collected via Yandex.Metrika strictly to analyze aggregated visit statistics and optimize website performance. See section 4 for details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Email Address:

  • To notify you about newly published books in the series “Engineering Model of Reality”
  • To reply to direct questions and feedback
  • Your email is never sold, leased, or transferred to third parties for marketing purposes

Aggregated Analytics:

  • To understand which sections of the methodology and diagnostic tools are most useful
  • To improve website performance, load speed, and navigational structure

3. Storage and Data Processors

Email: Forms are securely processed via Formspree (GDPR compliant) and delivered to dimaa@dimaa.ru. Only Dmitry Antonov has access to this mailbox.

Analytics: Stored on Yandex infrastructure in depersonalized form in accordance with the Yandex Privacy Policy.

4. Web Analytics & Cookie Management

The site runs a web analytics tag to gauge reach. It does not fingerprint individuals or track personal identity across third-party websites.

How to opt out:
  • Disable cookies in your browser settings.
  • Use privacy extensions (e.g. uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger).
  • Use the official opt-out tool: https://yandex.com/support/metrika/general/opt-out.html

5. Retention Period

Your email is retained until you unsubscribe or request data deletion. You can unsubscribe at any moment by clicking the unsubscribe link in any message or writing to dimaa@dimaa.ru.

6. How to Request Data Deletion

Send an email to dimaa@dimaa.ru with the subject "Delete My Data". We will remove your contact details within 3 business days.

Summary:

  • We only collect your email if you explicitly request book updates or contact us.
  • We never sell or share your data.
  • You can request full removal of your information at any time.
