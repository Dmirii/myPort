Why is there no "Book 4"?

Or rather, why it became Book 2.5: The Key to Mantic Practice

Between Book 2 (Navigation) and Book 3 of mantics, I realized a direct jump was too steep. Readers understood the theoretical concepts, but needed a 7-day hands-on bridge before beginning direct question work.

"Knowledge without practice is fragile. The transition required an intensive intermediate Key."

That intermediate bridge was published as Book 2.5: The Key to Mantic Practice (cataloged as Book 3 in the published sequence).
