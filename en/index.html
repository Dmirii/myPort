The Engineering Model of Reality — runes as the stages of a process

The Engineering Model of Reality is a system in which 24 runes describe the 24 stages of any process. I am Dmitry Antonov — a software engineer and practising runologist — and I have combined the logic of systems analysis with ancient knowledge.

The core thesis: runes are not just mystical signs — first and foremost they are a language for describing the stage of any process.

ᚠ Fehu — the beginning ᚺ Hagalaz — living with the result ᚦ Thurisaz — the choice ᚱ Raidho — the first step

At the heart of the Engineering Model of Reality are structure, observation and the management of stages: from washing the dishes to changing your career.
